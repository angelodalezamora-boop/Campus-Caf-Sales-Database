    Note: the data is sample data generated for this project with a fixed random seed (build_db.py). It is not real sales, so the interpretation shows analysis skill, not real business findings.

    Database design

    Six tables in third normal form. Lookup values (categories, payment methods) are stored once, and the price at the time of sale is kept in order_items so history stays correct if menu prices change.
